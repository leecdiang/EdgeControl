# EdgeControl 1.8.1

- Restores the 1.7.7 capsule dimensions: 144×40 pt, or 212×40 for an error.
- Makes the notch HUD slimmer: 24 pt below the physical cutout instead of 40, with narrower shoulders, smaller symbols, a 3 pt progress bar and tighter bottom corners.
- Keeps the notch silhouette flush with the display during its fade. Preview now uses the same geometry as the live HUD.
- Renames “Off / External” to “Off” and explains that hiding EdgeControl does not activate Alcove. This release does not add an Alcove bridge or synthetic media keys.

Gesture recognition, hardware writes and haptic scheduling are unchanged.
