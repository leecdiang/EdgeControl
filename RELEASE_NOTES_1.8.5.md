# EdgeControl 1.8.5

- Replaces the three-slider menu bar symbol with two rails representing the two trackpad edges; the menu panel uses the same template artwork.
- Refines the pearl-glass app icon with inward arrows connected to curved vertical gesture tracks, showing edge entry followed by vertical adjustment.
- Explicitly loads the installed bundle's app artwork for app-owned windows on launch.
- Connects the notch outline, wing movement and content fade to one reversible animation, removing the separate delayed text reveal.
- Keeps the HUD visible when a finger pauses during an active gesture; dismissal begins after lift. Rapid initial updates no longer bypass the collapsed first frame.

HUD dimensions, gesture recognition, haptic feedback and the repaired update feed are unchanged. Settings previews still dismiss automatically, and Reduce Motion keeps a short fade.
