# EdgeControl 1.8.3 validation

The supplied 11-second Alcove recording shows a horizontal expansion at menu-bar height, with the camera gap between a left icon/title and a right short progress indicator. The redesign follows that observed layout, not proprietary source or guessed private APIs. It removes the previous 24 pt strip below the notch. Each wing is 88 pt, progress is 62 × 4 pt, and the center follows NSScreen auxiliary areas in points. Frame sampling of the recording shows most width change in roughly 0.2 seconds, followed by a settling tail. Expansion/retraction uses a critically damped SwiftUI spring (response 0.32, dampingFraction 1) and keeps the window for 650 ms during retraction. These are EdgeControl design choices based on the observed motion, not a claim about Alcove's internals.

Screens with no valid cutout, inadequate wing space or a strip under 22 pt use a top capsule. Error messages also use a top capsule so text never passes under the camera. The original lower capsule remains 144 × 40 pt. Normal progress updates have no animation. Reduce Motion uses a fade. Pending presentation tasks are cancelled when changing modes or hiding, preventing a deferred entrance from reviving an old presentation.

151 XCTest methods include scaled/off-center/negative-origin geometry, camera exclusion, small-screen/error fallbacks, same-view mode switching, interrupted retraction and Reduce Motion. Native screenshots include all settings/menu/HUD surfaces in light and dark plus a transition contact sheet. CI results and screenshot review are recorded in the PR. Intermediate cached view bitmaps are supporting layout evidence, not a substitute for checking window-server animation on a physical notched Mac.

No device-control, brightness, gesture or haptic code changes. No Alcove notification injection or simulated media keys. Manual acceptance: inspect wings on the built-in screen, reverse a gesture during retraction, switch screens and HUD modes, and check Reduce Motion. Cross-model hardware validation remains outstanding; geometry coverage cannot guarantee every physical model.
