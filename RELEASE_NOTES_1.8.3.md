# EdgeControl 1.8.3

- Redesigns Notch HUD as two slim wings beside the physical camera cutout: icon and name on the left, progress on the right, with no extra strip below the notch.
- Adds curved shoulders and horizontal expansion/retraction. Progress remains directly driven by gesture values.
- Uses a readable top capsule for errors, notchless displays or insufficient space. Reduce Motion uses a short fade.
- Preserves the original 144 × 40 pt capsule, existing settings, brightness routing and haptics.

This is EdgeControl's own HUD, visually informed by the supplied Alcove recording. Off does not activate Alcove.
